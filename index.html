About Me

Hi! I’m Jack, a 20-year-old student at the University of Applied Sciences (HvA). I was born and raised in Amsterdam, and I’m half British, half Dutch—so I speak both languages fluently (though let’s be honest, who doesn’t nowadays?).

When it comes to skills, I have a strong foundation in both design and development. I'm proficient with tools like Figma, Miro, Adobe Photoshop, and Illustrator, and I’m comfortable navigating each stage of the design process—from desk research and ideation to prototyping, testing, and iterating.
